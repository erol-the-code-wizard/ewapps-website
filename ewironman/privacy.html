Privacy

Privacy Policy for EWIronman

Last updated: 5 September 2026

1. Overview

EWIronman is intended as a triathlon training and progress-tracking app.

2. Local data

User-entered training and planning data may be stored locally on your device.

3. Current website and app tracking

EWIronman does not include advertising. This website uses no analytics, cookies or third-party tracking. No external integrations are claimed by this policy.

4. Future integrations

If EWIronman later connects to third-party services or additional data sources, this privacy policy will be updated before those features are publicly released.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ewapps@t-online.de.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated as EWIronman’s functionality evolves. The updated version will be published on this page.
